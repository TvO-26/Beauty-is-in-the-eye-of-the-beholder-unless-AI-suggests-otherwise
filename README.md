# Beauty-is-in-the-eye-of-the-beholder-unless-AI-suggests-otherwise
Repository of dataset, code, and survey used in the research project 'Beauty is in the eye of the beholder, unless AI suggests otherwise'. 
Under 'data', the two anonymised datasets used in the statistical analyses of the paper are available. 
Under 'code', the python scripts used in various stages of the research are available. 
Under 'survey' a PDF export of the survey is available
Under 'corpus' the set of stimuli and the annotated dataset are available
